Keyboard Survival

Press the button to start the game.

How to Play

  • Use A to move left and D to move right.
  • Avoid the red obstacles falling from the top.
  • Collect green A-items and orange D-items to restore health.
  • Each obstacle you dodge increases your score.
  • When a key breaks, you can no longer use it until you collect its item.
